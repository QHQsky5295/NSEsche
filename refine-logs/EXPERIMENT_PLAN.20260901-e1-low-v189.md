# E1 Homogeneous-20 Low V189 Plan

V189 is the final currently authorized adaptive NSESche-only training test on
the revealed E1610--E1629 tapes. A pass can only authorize a fresh disjoint
confirmation; it cannot close the paper comparison.

V188 failed because its all-resident clearance term treated cold-, parent- and
data-blocked work as current CPU competition. It spread work to more starting
containers, doubled the positive cold-start share among completed functions,
lowered mean CPU utilization and lost throughput on all 20 paired seeds.

V189 makes one coefficient-free change. Every feasible node is ordered by an
NSESche-native earliest-executable-finish proxy:

`max(cold-start readiness, parent-transfer readiness) + equal-share CPU finish`.

The CPU term follows the simulator's equal-share discipline: each current
pending/runnable competitor contributes at most the current player's own work;
same-window projected players use the same cap. Blocked resident work is not
treated as current CPU contention. Cold start and parent transfer use a maximum
rather than a sum because they are parallel readiness gates. No queue router,
Borda vote, comparator score, completed-request outcome or fitted coefficient
is allowed.

Only 20 new NSESche runs are authorized. They reuse the exact V187 tapes and
immutable V187 control rows; no tape, control or baseline is rerun. The fixed
throughput and QPR gates are identical to V188, all valid observations must be
retained, and performance stays blind until the full 20-run inventory passes.

If V189 fails, this native earliest-finish axis closes and adaptive low-load
online search stops pending a broader review of the NSESche objective. Middle,
high and every later paper chapter remain blocked.
